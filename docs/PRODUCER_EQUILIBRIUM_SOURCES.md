# Producer's Equilibrium v27 source notes

## Official curriculum / examiner guidance
- CISCE ISC Economics syllabus (2026): Producer's equilibrium includes the profit-maximization meaning plus both TR-TC and MR-MC approaches with diagrams. The same syllabus separately lists equilibrium of a firm under perfect competition, including short-run break-even/shutdown and long-run equilibrium.
  https://cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/13.-ISC-Economics-XI-XII_020424_ISC2026.pdf
- CISCE 2025 Analysis of Pupil Performance: students commonly treated MR=MC as the sole condition; guidance says explain situations around both intersections and the economic implications of MC vs MR.
  https://cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/15.-ISC-Economics.pdf
- CISCE examiner analysis: TC should start from the Y-axis because TFC exists at zero output; distance between TR and TC shows profit; tangents can be used to identify maximum profit; loss diagrams need AVC and shutdown logic.
  https://devwebsite.cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Economics-1.pdf
