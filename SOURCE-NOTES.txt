CISCE evidence used while designing this chapter:
- 2024 Analysis of Pupil Performance: recommends real-life market examples, explanation of every market feature and its implication, clarity on firm vs industry, and familiarity with AR/demand curves in different markets.
- CISCE specimen material includes identification of monopoly from a sole-producer scenario and comparisons of monopolistic competition with oligopoly using product type, entry/exit and price control.
- Earlier CISCE examiner material compares perfect and monopolistic competition using product nature, price influence, AR/MR relationship and demand curve.
